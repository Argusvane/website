The name

You have seen this eye before.

It is on every peacock feather. The Greeks said it was put there.

A hundred eyes One direction

Argus

It belonged to Argus, who had a hundred and was set to guard what mattered.

He was never caught looking away.

When he was gone, Hera kept the eyes and gave them to her bird.

Vane

The blade of a feather is called a vane.

So is the arrow on the roof that turns before the storm does.

An eye that does not close. An arrow that does not guess.
