Capabilities

Three capabilities

Most engagements begin with reconnaissance and grow into a build. You can task any one of these on its own.

Reconnaissance

Before we write code, we map the ground. We study your processes, your codebase and your data, then report what to build, fix or retire first.

  • Codebase and architecture assessments
  • Process and requirements analysis
  • Technical due diligence

Development

We design and build systems against the requirements we set together, and we integrate them with the tools your teams already use.

  • Purpose-built applications
  • Integrations and data pipelines
  • Dashboards and internal tools

Watch

We keep watch over systems in production. We read their signals, fix what degrades and hand them over with documentation when your team is ready.

  • Monitoring and telemetry
  • Performance and cost reviews
  • Training and handover

Development cycle

From requirement to release

Every project follows the same five stages, in this order. Each one ends with a product you can read or run.

  1. 01

    Direction

    We define the mission with you. Its objectives become the requirements that guide all later work.

  2. 02

    Collection

    We gather from every source: code, data, logs, documents and the people who use the system each day.

  3. 03

    Analysis

    We weigh the evidence and brief you on a design, with the trade-offs and our confidence in each choice.

  4. 04

    Delivery

    We build in short cycles and deploy early. You see working code, and reports from the field shape the next release.

  5. 05

    Watch

    We monitor what is in production, read its signals, and feed what we learn back into Direction.

Doctrine

The engineering standards we work to

Sourced decisions

Every design decision cites the evidence behind it. You can check our reasoning.

Stated confidence

We say how sure we are of each estimate and what new information would change it.

Verified before fielded

Nothing reaches production until it has been tested and independently reviewed.

Yours to keep

The code, the data and the documentation belong to you, and we build so that another team could take over.
